Brand identity · v1

Comfyture is Comfy plus confiture, the French word for jam. It is lettered, never typeset, and its first letter wears a fitted jar lid.

01 · Story

Comfy, plus a jar of jam

The name is a pun. In French, jam and preserves are confiture. Swap the con for com, the fi for fy, and you have Comfyture: a desk for making shots, named like something you put up in a jar and keep.

From the shelf to the deskconfiture↓comfyture

Comfy is for ComfyUI, the engine it runs on. -ture is for the jar: the thing that keeps a shot until you are ready to open it.

The brief was a jam label meets creative software: the warmth of a well-printed European preserve, drawn like a modern tool. The lid on the C is where the two meet.

The lid

the lidthe C

The first letter wears a fitted, curved jar lid that follows the upper contour of the C. It is a separate shape, a small gap away, so the C reads as a vessel and the lid as the thing you lift.

That is why the lid is never left out and never joined to the C: it is the one detail that says jar.

Colour, and where it came from

Acid yellow#F2FF59ComfyUI’s yellow

Comfyture is built on ComfyUI, so it keeps its lemon-yellow as the one accent: the same colour that lights “selected” and “go” in the app. On the jar it is the label.

Charcoal & ink#121214 · #211927The product’s own surfaces

The app’s dark panels carry over unchanged, so the brand and the product feel like one object. Ink is the warmer cousin used on pages and covers.

Plum#49378BThe jam

The only colour the product doesn’t have: the fruit. Used sparingly, as a second ground for the wordmark, never as a UI colour.

Comfyture is an independent, open-source project. It uses ComfyUI’s yellow as a nod to where it runs; it does not use, imitate or alter ComfyUI’s own logo.

02 · The system

Three marks, one hand

The wordmark is the signature. The mark is its first letter with the lid, for places that are square or small. The app icon is the mark on its tile. They share one set of curves, traced from the approved lettering.

Wordmark

Signature. Headers, documents, the README, the demo end card. Default colour is yellow on ink.

Mark

The C with its lid. Two shapes: the lid is part of the mark and is never left out, never joined to the C.

App icon

The tile. Mark in ink on yellow, corners at 22.5 % of the side. Used as favicon, dock icon and store icon.

03 · Space and size

Room to breathe

Keep a margin of x on every side, where x is the height of the lid. Nothing enters it: no text, no edges, no other marks.
