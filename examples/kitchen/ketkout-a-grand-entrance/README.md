# Ketkout — A Grand Entrance

A 34-second Kitchen film: Ketkout bursts into his tiny kitchen, spots the Comfyture jar, bakes a jam tart and opens the door to his friends.
It is the whole Kitchen chain in one project: the story with its cast and place, eleven shots each with its start frame and its take, and
the finished edit.

Open **Kitchen → Ketkout — A Grand Entrance**. The story shows every shot with the frame and the prompt that made it; **Cut** opens the
edit (eleven clips on the video track, two overlays) and plays the exported film. Browsing the project and playing its media spend nothing.
Duplicate the story before making your own version.

## How it was made

- **Idea:** an animated short, punchy and playful: *"A dramatic entrance, tiny determined footsteps, one irresistible jar and a
  tart-shaped idea."* The cast (Ketkout, Crumpet, Meringue) and the place (Ketkout's kitchen) come from their reference pictures.
- **Frames:** each shot's start frame was drawn with GPT Image 2.5 Flare from those references, so the room, the light and the
  characters stay the same from shot to shot.
- **Takes:** every shot was rolled with LTX-2.5 from its frame, picture and sound together, two to six seconds each.
- **Cut:** the takes were trimmed and joined in Cut and exported as one film, 1280 × 704.
