How these keyframes were made

  • Captured on 25 October in headless Chromium at 1920×1080, on a lab server with a fresh data folder, no Comfy key and a dead render address: no credit was spent and every request outside the machine was blocked.
  • Frames of our cast's real takes (Ketkout, the otter, Meringue) were grabbed with ffmpeg. Everything shown is our own cast: no real people, no other creator's media.
  • Staged frames: Crumpet's answer was written by hand; the page checked and applied it as it would a model's.
  • Kitchen's (beat 3), Toaster's (beat 4), Jam's (beat 6) and the tour's (beat 12) frames were captured again on the shipped examples on 25 October, on a fresh install. Macaron's (beat 8) can still be refreshed on its Content workspace.
  • Never on screen: the Giorno project or anything of data/toasts; the Toc Toc and Hotel Lobby showcases; any trend's original sound; a key or the header's balance.
